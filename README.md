A single fortune cookie line for a crypto trader who has just been liquidated: oracular on the outside, a joke on the loss at the last word. `main.py` scores 13 hand-written candidate lines against the judge's brief, revises the winner once by hand, and writes the fortune to `/output/entry.txt`:

> The universe took your money and left you a story, a punchline, and this cookie.

15 words, 80 characters, one line — inside the 20-word cap and the 160-character gate.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
